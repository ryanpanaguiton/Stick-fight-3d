Stick Fight 3D

Best of three rounds. Block, parry, roll, strike.

Player 1WASD move (W/S go in and out of the screen), Space jump, J light, K heavy, L kick, U special, Shift block, Q roll
Player 2Arrows move, Numpad 0 jump, 1 light, 2 heavy, 3 kick, 4 special, 5 block, 6 roll (number row works too)
Tap block right as a hit lands to parry it and stun the attacker. Blocking drains stamina; empty stamina breaks your guard.
P pause, R restart, M mute, Esc menu
